Schemes and Programmes Overview (general guidance - verify details on the official portal)
kind: Scheme
Skill development schemes: government skill programmes offer short-term, free or subsidised training with assessment and a certificate. Eligibility usually depends on age, education and whether you are currently in a job. Documents commonly asked for are Aadhaar, a bank account, an education certificate and a passport photo. Check the official scheme portal for current dates and rules.
Cooperative entrepreneurship support: rural learners who want to start or join a cooperative, self-help group or farmer producer organisation can look for support through state cooperative departments, NABARD-linked programmes and local cooperative federations. Typical support includes training, group formation help and access to credit. Eligibility, benefits and deadlines differ by state, so confirm with the district cooperative office.
Dairy and agriculture cooperatives: dairy cooperative unions and agricultural credit societies regularly need accountants, secretaries and field officers. Training in cooperative accounting, record keeping and member management improves your chances.
Women and youth focused programmes: several state and national programmes encourage women-led self-help groups and young entrepreneurs through training and credit linkage. Ask your institution or district cooperative office which are open now.
