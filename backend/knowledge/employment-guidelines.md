Jobs and internships on CoopConnect are posted by registered employers such as cooperative banks, dairy unions, credit societies and federations. The match percentage shows how many of the skills required for a role appear in your skills and certificates. A higher match does not guarantee selection. Employers make their own hiring decisions.
To improve your match, complete courses that teach the missing skills and keep your profile and skills up to date. Learners who mark themselves open to work become visible to employers. Always check the job page for the deadline and the employer's own requirements before applying.
