Cooperative Basics Guide
kind: Handbook
A cooperative is a member-owned organisation run on the principle of one member, one vote. Primary Agricultural Credit Societies provide credit to farmers, dairy cooperatives collect and market milk, and cooperative banks serve members and local businesses. Roles include secretary, manager, accountant, field officer and board member.
Cooperative accounting covers member share capital, loans and deposits, audit and annual reports. Cooperative management covers governance, meetings, member communication and legal compliance under the relevant Cooperative Societies Act. Digital literacy helps with online payments, record keeping and government portals.
