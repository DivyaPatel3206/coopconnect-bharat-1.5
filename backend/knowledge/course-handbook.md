Each course has lessons in video, reading or quiz form. Mark a lesson complete to move your progress forward. Quizzes have a pass mark, usually 60 percent, and can be attempted again. If you fail a quiz, revisit the lesson and try again. Assignments are submitted inside the course and graded by the trainer.
When all lessons are complete the course reaches 100 percent and a certificate is issued automatically. Courses are available in English, and some also in Hindi or Gujarati. Beginners are advised to start with Digital Literacy before moving to accounting or management courses.
